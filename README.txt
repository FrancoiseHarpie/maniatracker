Sonic Mania Plus Archipelago Map tracker
VERSION 1.0

What's in there?
- Features tracking of Characters (sans &Knuckles for now), Chaos Emeralds, Abilities, Acts, Special Stages and Blue Sphere stages unlocks 
- All checks from Mania Mode : Act Clears, Giant Rings, Special Stages
- Blue Sphere checks
- Achievementsanity checks
- Full level maps for each Mania mode Act
- Extra map to keep track of individual Special Stage, Blue Spheres level and Achievement clears
- Supported options : Blue Sphere (off, clear, perfect), Actsanity (on, off), Achievementsanity (on, off)

What to expect from next version?
- Encore mode checks and maps
- &Knuckles and &Tails tracking
- Elemental Shields
- Monitorsanity (have mercy!)
